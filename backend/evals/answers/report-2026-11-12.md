| Is my monthly salary counted as taxable income? | §12(1), §12(2) | answered | §12(1): Any salary received by an employee in a tax year, other than salary that is exempt from tax under this Ordinance, is chargeable to tax in that year under the head “Salary”. |

| Is the medical allowance my company pays part of my salary for tax? | §12(2) | answered | §12(2): Salary includes the amount of any allowance provided by an employer to an employee, such as a cost of living, subsistence, rent, utilities, education, entertainment, or travel allowance. However, it does not include any allowance solely expended in the performance of the employee's duties of employment. |
| My employer pays my income tax for me. Does that count as extra income? | §12(3) | answered | §12(3): When an employer agrees to pay the tax chargeable on an employee's salary, the amount of the employee's income chargeable under the head Salary shall be grossed up by the amount of tax payable by the employer. |
| Can I deduct my commuting costs from my salary income? | §12(4) | answered | §12(4): No deduction shall be allowed for any expenditure incurred by an employee in deriving amounts chargeable to tax under the head Salary. |
| I got salary arrears from two years ago. Can they be taxed at the old rates? | §12(7), §12(8) | answered | §12(7), ch. 6.1: Where salary is paid in arrears and results in the employee being chargeable at higher rates of tax than would have applied if paid in the tax year services were rendered, the employee may elect by notice in writing to the Commissioner for the amount to be taxed at those rates. To avail a lower tax rate as per section 12(7) when arrears pertain to previous tax years, you can enter the amount in the Subject to Final Tax column under the Employment section in the return. |
| Is my pension taxed as salary? | §12(2A), §12(2), §149(1A) | answered | §12(2): Salary includes any pension or annuity, or any supplement to a pension or annuity, received by an employee from any employment. |
| Is money my old employer paid me after I left still salary? | §12(2), §12(5) | answered | §12(2), §12(5): Salary includes any amount received by an employee from any employment of a revenue or capital nature. This includes any amount received on termination of employment, whether paid voluntarily or under an agreement, including compensation for redundancy or loss of employment and golden handshake payments. Furthermore, an amount is treated as received from employment regardless of whether it is paid by a past employer. |
| My company gave me a car that I also use on weekends. Is that taxed? | §13(3) | answered | §13(3): Where a motor vehicle is provided by an employer to an employee wholly or partly for private use in a tax year, the amount chargeable to tax under the head Salary shall include an amount computed as prescribed. This applies when the vehicle is provided partly for private use, such as on weekends. |
| How is free housing from my employer valued for tax? | §13(12) | answered | §13(12): Where accommodation or housing is provided by an employer to an employee in the tax year, the amount chargeable to tax under the head Salary shall include an amount computed as may be prescribed. |
| My employer pays my electricity and gas bills. Is that income? | §13(6) | answered | §13(6): Where utilities are provided by an employer to an employee in a tax year, the amount chargeable to tax under the head Salary shall include the fair market value of the utilities provided, reduced by any payment made by the employee for them. |
| My company provides a driver for me. Do I pay tax on that? | §13(5) | answered | §13(5): Where the services of a driver are provided by an employer to an employee in a tax year, the amount chargeable to tax under the head Salary shall include the total salary paid to the driver for services rendered to the employee, as reduced by any payment made to the employer for such services. |
| I took an interest-free loan from my employer. Is there tax on it? | §13(7), §13(14) | answered | §13(7): Where an employer makes a loan on or after the 1 st day of July, 2002 to an employee with no profit on loan payable, the amount chargeable to tax under the head Salary shall include an amount equal to the profit on loan computed at the benchmark rate. However, this sub-section shall not apply to loans not exceeding one millionrupees. |
| My boss wrote off money I owed the company. Does that count as income? | §13(9), §13(10) | answered | §13(9): When an employer waives an employee's obligation to pay or repay an amount owing, the amount chargeable to tax under the head Salary for that tax year includes the amount waived. This means the waived amount counts as taxable income. |
| I bought a laptop from my company below market price. Is the discount taxed? | §13(11) | answered | §13(11), §13(1): Where property is transferred by an employer to an employee, the amount chargeable to tax under the head Salary shall include the fair market value of the property determined at the time it is transferred, as reduced by any payment made by the employee for the property. |
| How are benefits from my job that aren't cash valued? | §13(1), §13(13) | answered | §13(13): When an employer provides an employee with a perquisite that is not covered by sub-sections (3) through (12), the amount chargeable to tax under the head Salary shall include the fair market value of the perquisite determined at the time it is provided, except where the rules provide otherwise, as reduced by any payment made by the employee for the perquisite. |
| Why does my employer cut tax from my pay every month? | §149(1) | answered | §149(1): Every person responsible for paying salary to an employee must deduct tax at the time of payment. This deduction is made from the amount paid at the employee's average rate of tax computed on their estimated salary income for the tax year. |
| How does my company work out how much tax to take from each paycheck? | §149(1), §149(2) | answered | §149(2), §149(1): Every person responsible for paying salary to an employee shall deduct tax at the time of payment at the employee's average rate of tax. This rate is computed on the estimated income chargeable under the head Salary for the tax year using the formula A/B. Component A is the tax payable on component B as taxable income, and component B is the estimated salary income plus tax chargeable under section 4AB. |

| Is the profit on my savings account taxed? | §7B(1), §151(1) | answered | §151(1), §7B(1): When a banking company or financial institution pays profit on a debt or deposit, the payer deducts tax from the gross amount reduced by Zakat. A tax is also imposed on persons other than a company receiving profit on debt from specified entities. |
| Does my bank keep some of my savings profit as tax? | §151(1) | answered | §151(1): When a banking company or financial institution pays profit on a debt or deposit, the payer of the profit must deduct tax at the rate specified in Division IA of Part III of the First Schedule from the gross amount. This deduction is made from the gross amount reduced by the amount of Zakat, if any, paid under the Zakat and Ushr Ordinance, 1980 (XVII of 1980), at the time the profit is paid. |
| Are National Savings certificate returns taxed? | §151(1), §7B | answered | §151(1), §7B(2): When a person pays yield on an account, deposit, or certificate under the National Savings Scheme, the payer shall deduct tax from the gross amount of the yield or profit paid as reduced by the amount of Zakat. The tax imposed on a person other than a company who receives a profit on debt shall be computed by applying the relevant rate of tax to the gross amount of the profit on debt. |
| Is the tax my bank takes on profit the last tax I owe on it? | §151(3), §7B(2) | answered | §151(1), §7B(1): A banking company paying profit on a debt or deposit must deduct tax from the gross amount as reduced by the amount of Zakat at the time the profit is paid. A tax is imposed on every person, other than a company, who receives a profit on debt from such a payer. |
