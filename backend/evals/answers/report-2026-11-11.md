| My employer pays my income tax for me. Does that count as extra income? | §12(3) | answered | §12(3): Where an employer agrees to pay the tax chargeable on an employee’s salary, the amount of the employee’s income chargeable under the head Salary shall be grossed up by the amount of tax payable by the employer. |

You can also enter the arrears amount pertaining to previous tax years in the designated column of the return to avail a lower tax rate than the current tax year. |
| Is my pension taxed as salary? | §12(2A), §12(2), §149(1A) | answered | §12(2), ch. 6.1: Salary includes any pension or annuity, or any supplement to a pension or annuity, under section 12(2). | Is money my old employer paid me after I left still salary? | §12(2), §12(5) | answered | §12(2), §12(5): Salary means any amount received by an employee from any employment, whether of a revenue or capital nature. This includes any amount received on termination of employment, whether paid voluntarily or under an agreement, including any compensation for redundancy or loss of employment and golden handshake payments. |
| My company gave me a car that I also use on weekends. This applies when the vehicle is provided in a tax year. |
| How is free housing from my employer valued for tax? | §13(12) | answered | §13(12): Where accommodation or housing is provided by an employer to an employee in the tax year, the amount chargeable to tax under the head Salary shall include an amount computed as may be prescribed. |
| My employer pays my electricity and gas bills. Is that income? | §13(6) | answered | §13(6): Where utilities are provided by an employer to an employee in a tax year, the amount chargeable to tax under the head Salary shall include the fair market value of the utilities provided, reduced by any payment made by the employee for the utilities. |
| My company provides a driver for me. Do I pay tax on that? | §13(5) | answered | §13(5): When an employer provides the services of a driver to an employee in a tax year, the amount chargeable to tax under the head Salary includes the total salary paid to that driver for services rendered, reduced by any payment made to the employer for such services. |
| I took an interest-free loan from my employer. Is there tax on it? | §13(7), §13(14) | answered | §13(7): Where an employer makes a loan on or after the 1 st day of July, 2002, and no profit on loan is payable by the employee, the amount chargeable to tax under the head Salary shall include an amount equal to the profit on loan computed at the benchmark rate. However, this sub-section shall not apply to loans not exceeding one millionrupees. |
| My boss wrote off money I owed the company. Does that count as income? | §13(9), §13(10) | answered | §13(9): When an employer waives an employee's obligation to pay or repay an amount owing to the employer in a tax year, the amount chargeable to tax under the head Salary shall include the waived amount. Therefore, the waived amount counts as taxable income. |
| I bought a laptop from my company below market price. Is the discount taxed? | §13(11) | answered | §13(11), §13(1): When property is transferred by an employer to an employee, the amount chargeable to tax under the head Salary shall include the fair market value of the property determined at the time it is transferred, reduced by any payment made by the employee. Therefore, the difference between the fair market value and your payment is included in your taxable salary. |
| How are benefits from my job that aren't cash valued? | §13(1), §13(13) | answered | §13(13): Where an employer provides an employee with a perquisite that is not covered by sub-sections (3) through (12), the amount chargeable to tax under the head Salary shall include the fair market value of the perquisite. This value is determined at the time it is provided, except where the rules provide otherwise, and is reduced by any payment made by the employee for the perquisite. |
| Why does my employer cut tax from my pay every month? | §149(1) | answered | §149(1): Every person responsible for paying salary to an employee must deduct tax from the amount paid at the time of payment. This deduction is calculated using the employee's average rate of tax computed on their estimated salary income for the tax year. |
| How does my company work out how much tax to take from each paycheck? | §149(1), §149(2) | answered | §149(2), §149(1): Every person responsible for paying salary to an employee shall deduct tax at the time of payment at the employee's average rate of tax. This rate is computed on the estimated income of the employee chargeable under the head Salary for the tax year using the formula A/B. Component A is the tax payable if the amount in component B were the taxable income, and component B is the estimated salary income plus tax chargeable under section 4AB. |
