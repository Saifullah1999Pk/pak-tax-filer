# 004 Deterministic Tax Engine: Design

**Date:** 2026-11-03 · **Status:** Proposed · **Feature:** 004 (README milestone 4) ·
**Governed by:** [Constitution](../../../.specify/memory/constitution.md)

## Intent

README milestone 4: "Deterministic tax engine: real TY2026 rates sourced from FBR directly,
salary + §151 calculation with explanation." From a user's confirmed certificates (feature 003)
and ATL status, calculate their TY2026 tax in plain code, show every step, and say clearly what
is owed or over-withheld. Every rate comes from FBR's own law text (Principle II); nothing is
computed by an LLM (Principle I).

## Sources (verified 2026-11-03)

All from the **Income Tax Ordinance, 2001, as amended up to 31.07.2025** (FBR:
`download1.fbr.gov.pk/Docs/2025881983148210Income-Tax-Ordinance,-2001-Amended-upto-31.07.2025.pdf`),
which carries the Finance Act 2025 changes for TY2026.

| Rule | Location | Content |
|---|---|---|
| Salaried slabs | First Schedule, Part I, Division I, clause (2), printed p. 492 (substituted by Finance Act 2025) | ≤ 600,000: 0 · 600,001–1,200,000: 1% over 600,000 · 1,200,001–2,200,000: 6,000 + 11% over 1,200,000 · 2,200,001–3,200,000: 116,000 + 23% over 2,200,000 · 3,200,001–4,100,000: 346,000 + 30% over 3,200,000 · > 4,100,000: 616,000 + 35% over 4,100,000 |
| Salaried-table condition | same clause | applies where salary exceeds 75% of taxable income |
| Surcharge | §4AB, proviso added by Finance Act 2025 | 9% of the Division I tax for salaried individuals whose taxable income exceeds Rs 10 million |
| Profit on debt | §7B(1)–(2) | separate tax on the **gross** profit on debt of a non-company, at the Division IIIA rate |
| Profit-on-debt rate | First Schedule, Part I, Division IIIA(a) (substituted by Finance Act 2025) | 20% of profit paid by a banking company on an account or deposit |
| Profit-on-debt limit | §7B(3)(b) | §7B does not apply where profit on debt exceeds Rs 5 million |
| Rounding | §219 | tax or refund to the nearest rupee; fractions of 50 paisa or more round up |

**Status:** the slab table was located in the Ordinance text by Saifullah and cross-checked
against the circular and by arithmetic; the other rows were read from the Ordinance text. Hand
verification of the worked examples (and so of every calculation) is still pending and is the
**first implementation task**: no engine code is written until those examples are signed off.
**FBR Circular 01 of 2025-26 has two wrong slab amounts** (345,000 and 615,000); the Ordinance's 346,000 and 616,000 are the only
values consistent with the bands (116,000 + 23% × 1,000,000 = 346,000; 346,000 + 30% × 900,000 =
616,000), and the statute prevails. The superseded tables printed as footnotes (Finance Acts
2023 and 2024) must never be used.

## Scope

**Inputs:** the user's confirmed certificates for TY2026 (from 003's `confirmed_documents`),
summed per type; ATL status from the user's taxpayer profile (feature 002; amended
2026-11-04, previously asked on the tax page).

**Calculation:**
1. **Salary tax:** apply the salaried slabs to the summed taxable salary.
2. **Surcharge:** if taxable income exceeds Rs 10 million, add 9% of the salary tax.
3. **Profit-on-debt tax:** 20% of summed gross bank profit (§7B).
4. **Credits:** tax deducted by employers and tax withheld by banks, summed from certificates.
5. **Result:** salary side: (salary tax + surcharge) − tax deducted = **payable** or
   **over-deducted**; profit side: §7B tax − tax withheld.
6. **Rounding:** exact `Decimal` arithmetic throughout; final tax and balance amounts rounded per
   §219.

**Explanation (approach C, decided by Saifullah):**
- An **authoritative step-by-step breakdown** generated by code from templates, always shown
  ("Your taxable salary of Rs 1,800,000 falls in the 1.2m–2.2m band: Rs 6,000 + 11% of Rs
  600,000 = Rs 72,000"), each step citing its source row.
- A **short plain-language summary by Claude**, written from the breakdown. Before display,
  code extracts every number from the summary and checks each against the breakdown; on any
  mismatch, or if the API is unavailable, the summary is dropped and only the breakdown is
  shown (Principle I: no new figures).

**Rates module:** all rates live in `backend/app/tax/rates/` as data keyed by tax year, each with
its source document, location and the Finance Act that set it; guarded by 001's hook. Adding a
tax year means adding data, not changing code.

## Provisional rules (pending the lead's answers, asked in the PR)

1. **Profit on debt over Rs 5 million:** §7B stops applying; the MVP refuses with a clear message
   rather than guess the alternative treatment.
2. **Non-ATL users:** banks withhold 40% but the §7B tax is 20%. The difference is shown as
   **"extra tax withheld; whether it can be reclaimed needs confirmation"**, never as a refund.
3. **75% salary condition:** checked twice, with and without §7B profit in taxable income. If
   both say salary exceeds 75%, calculate; if they disagree, show "this situation needs a tax
   practitioner" instead of a number. The same double check applies to the Rs 10 million
   surcharge threshold.
4. **Combining balances:** salary and profit balances are shown separately, with no combined
   total, since it isn't clear whether extra bank withholding can be set off against salary tax
   owed.

## Out of scope

Other income, deductions, tax credits (e.g. donations, Zakat), allowances analysis (the
certificate's taxable salary is used as stated), pension, profit on debt from sources other than
bank deposits (e.g. National Savings), tax years other than TY2026 (structure supports them),
filing.

## Approaches (explanation)

| Approach | Trade-off | Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| A. Template breakdown only | Always correct and free; mechanical; doesn't use Claude as the README expects | Kept as the authoritative part of C |
| B. Claude writes the explanation | Natural; can misstate or invent numbers | Rejected |
| C. Template breakdown + Claude summary with a number check | Natural and safe; summary dropped on any mismatch | **Chosen** |

## Decisions

- **Test-first from worked examples** (Principle VI): each band, both band edges, the surcharge
  threshold, the Rs 5m limit, §219 rounding at exactly 50 paisa, and multiple certificates
  summed, each computed by hand from the cited source and written as a test before the code.
  *(Builder's call.)*
- **Self-check test:** for every tax year in the rates module, each band's fixed amount must
  equal the tax at the top of the previous band, which catches typos like the circular's.
  *(Builder's call.)*
- **Pure function core:** `calculate(inputs, rates) -> breakdown`, with no database or network,
  so it is trivially testable and reusable by the MCP tool `calculate_income_tax` (006).
  *(Builder's call.)*
- **Not ready to calculate** when the user has no taxpayer profile, no confirmed salary certificate
  exists, or a provisional rule blocks; the user is told why. *(Builder's call.)*
- **Not tax advice:** results carry the "not yet practitioner-reviewed" label (Principle IV).

## Done when

- Every rate in the module cites its source, and the self-check test passes.
- All worked-example tests pass, written before the code.
- A user with confirmed certificates sees tax, credits and balance with a step-by-step breakdown.
- The Claude summary never shows a number absent from the breakdown (tested with a summary that
  contains a wrong number).
